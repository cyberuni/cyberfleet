Part of the [cyberfleet plugin](/cyberfleet/overview/). See that page for install instructions.

**Trigger:** invoked when the Council wants to **recruit, browse, or discharge specialist crew**: a marketplace persona type. Not for spawning a ship instance ([Operator](/cyberfleet/operator/) today), building or tuning an automaton ([Mechanic](/cyberfleet/mechanic/)), or authoring a plain workflow skill from scratch.

Specialist crew don't come bundled. The **Crimp** recruits them from the [**Tavern**](https://cyberuni.github.io/cyberplace/tavern/), the storefront of installable **specialist crew** (aced, quill, Warden, Scanner). Each one keeps its own face, and you hail it by name. Browse the roster, pick the hands you need, and the Crimp signs them on.

## What it does

- Browses the Tavern roster of installable specialist crew.
- Signs a chosen specialist onto the fleet: installs its persona so you can command it.
- Discharges a specialist that's no longer needed.
