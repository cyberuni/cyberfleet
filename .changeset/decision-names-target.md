`authority-governance` §3: a turn whose words name the action and its target ("merge #75") is now a Council decision on its own, with no confirming round trip. It covers that target at the revision it had when the words arrived, so a push before the action means asking again. A bare "Approve" with no decision-request outstanding still covers nothing. The Pod's merge-on-the-Council's-word step uses the new rule.
