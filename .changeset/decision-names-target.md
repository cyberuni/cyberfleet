`authority-governance` §3: a turn whose words name the action and its target ("merge #75") is now a Council decision on its own, with no confirming round trip. It covers that target at the revision it had when the words arrived, so a push before the action means asking again. A bare "Approve" with no decision-request outstanding still covers nothing. An order that asks for its work's merges is the standing form of the same rule (§7). §7 gains a pod carve-out that is not a transfer: a pod may merge its own pull request on the Council's own words naming that merge in the pod's session, never on mail, a comment, or a claim, and a dispatcher never relays a merge to a pod.
