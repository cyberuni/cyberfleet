`cyberfleet jump <message-id>` reads the sender's origin a message records (from cyberlegion's next release): it focuses the recorded pane while it is open, and when the pane has closed it names the recorded repo, branch, and worktree even after the sender's unit record is pruned. A message with `blocked: true` carries that mark in the result, and `/jump` says the sender is waiting on the Council. Messages without an origin resolve through the sender's live record as before.
