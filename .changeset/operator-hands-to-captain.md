Operator spawns no Pods. To put work on a project it reads that project's Captain with `cyberfleet captain`, starts it in the project's default checkout when it is not healthy, or hands the order to the healthy owner, and takes over nothing by being called. Writing Pod briefs, announcing and gating merges, merging, and retiring Pods move to the Captain, and the authority governance now names the Captain as the one that merges under a Council delegation.
